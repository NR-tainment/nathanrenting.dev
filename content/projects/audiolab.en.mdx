## Three principles (from the site itself)

1. **Tools you can actually use.** Each cluster ships
   a working, browser-side demo. Nothing hidden behind a
   waitlist.
2. **People-first writing.** No keyword-stuffed AI slop. Every page
   is written by someone who has shipped audio in production and
   is willing to be wrong in public.
3. **Built where it matters.** Android, realtime DSP, accessibility,
   developer ergonomics. The places where audio AI actually has to work,
   not just marketing demos.

## Six labs — honest about what works

The site revolves around six browser-side labs. Each lab is honestly labelled by
what it actually does — working, in progress, or a simulated concept:

| Lab | Status | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **[MixLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/mixlab)** | Beta | Loudness/dynamics/stereo || **verified to ±0.08 LU against the EBU R128 reference** |
| **[VoiceLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/voicelab)** | Beta | Voice QA: clarity, pacing, filler density, clipping |
| **[SignalLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/signallab)** | Beta | Audio indexer: silence/clipping detection, region tags |
| **[Ψ Lab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/psilab)** | Building | Phase vocoder || time-stretch, freeze, reverse, smear (fully client-side AudioWorklet) |
| **[SkillLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/skilllab)** | Building | Practice challenges with feedback (works, being expanded) |
| **[HearLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/hearlab)** | Building | Non-medical hearing companion (dashboard separate as a concept) |
| **[CueLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/cuelab)** | Simulated | Live-routing concept with simulated data |

Everything client-side: audio never leaves the browser. Alongside that, docs,
methodology, glossary, roadmap — indexable content, no JS-only routes.

lots of static pages, React islands for interactive
demo tools only where it's truly needed. No 100% client-side
rendering for a glossary.

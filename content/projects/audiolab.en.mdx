## What it is

1. **160 lenses, each with a status.** Each lens says what it measures, how to
   read it and how far to trust it, with a margin where one was measured.
2. **Independent.** No recogniser or noise suppressor to sell, so no stake in
   the outcome.
3. **Checked.** Loudness within 0.06 LU on test signals built in-house from
   EBU Tech 3341/3342 and BS.1770-4, inside the 0.1 LU the standard allows.

<ChalkDoodle type="arrow-curve" className="mt-12 mb-2 opacity-50" />

## Seven labs, free in your browser

Each lab answers one question about your audio, except CueLab, a routing
sandbox that measures nothing. Your audio is not uploaded. One exception:
HearLab's live captions use your browser's speech recognition, which may send
audio to an online service (Chrome does). The same measurement engine also
answers in the API and the MCP server.

| Lab | Status | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **[MixLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/mixlab)** | Beta | How loud is my mix, and how much headroom does it leave? |
| **[VoiceLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/voicelab)** | Beta | Is this voice take usable, and is that note in tune? |
| **[SignalLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/signallab)** | Live | What is in this recording: speech, music, noise or silence, and where? |
| **[HearLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/hearlab)** | Beta | Log a hard hearing moment with a measurement of the sound. A non-medical companion to your hearing care. |
| **[CueLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/cuelab)** | Beta | Map your stream setup (sources, routes, cues) and check it before you go live. A demo for now. |
| **[SkillLab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/skilllab)** | Beta | Practise sound design, speaking, recording and singing, with feedback. |
| **[Ψ Lab](https://audiolab.tools/labs/psilab)** | Beta | Reshape the time of a sound: stretch it, freeze it, run it backward. |

- Four languages: English, Dutch, German and French

# AudioLaunch — mobile music studio + Resonara

<MarkerHighlight>A mobile music studio with a custom native Rust audio engine, plus Resonara: a gamified world that teaches sound design while you play.</MarkerHighlight>{" "}
Eight years solo-built — from the first concept in 2018 to today's
public open beta. Until recently this listed here under Stealth; the
name is off now.

<ChalkDoodle type="x-mark" className="mt-12 mb-2 opacity-50" />

## Two sides

**Studio** is the serious side: multi-track recording up to
24-bit/96kHz, live editing, FX chain, MIDI, sample import, synths,
mixer, master chain. Runs on a custom native Rust audio engine for
low-latency real-time DSP on Android (NDK + JNI + React Native FFI).

**Resonara** is the other side: a story-driven world that teaches
sound design through missions. Vibes, worlds, level pacing, missions
and skills as progression mechanics — the play is the curriculum.

## Status

Live in public open beta at [audio-launch.com](https://audio-launch.com) —
play in the browser or grab the Android build. Not yet on the regular
Play Store listing; that's the next step. Build philosophy: SOTA-or-cut
— features that can't ship at SOTA quality are cut from v1.0 rather
than shipped half-baked.

Sister project: [audiolab.tools](https://audiolab.tools), a public
browser-side audio toolkit — a separate product, also by me.
